# Code of Conduct

We want rxpress to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and propose changes without being treated badly for it.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and be patient with people who are new to the project or to the topic.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and take it the same way.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, open a private report with **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's Security tab and say that it is a conduct report. It stays between you and the maintainer.
